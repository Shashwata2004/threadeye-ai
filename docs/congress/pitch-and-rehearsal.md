# M9 — presentation kit (preparation, not an event-completion claim)

M7 model improvement and M8 final-submission/video production are outside the current request.
Repository publication and release publishing are also excluded by the owner.

## Three-minute live pitch

- 0:00–0:25 — A receiving inspector must catch faults before cutting. ThreadEye adds assistance to
  an existing inspection machine. Say that the shown video is simulated and identify the active detector.
- 0:25–1:10 — Load a labelled roll, show moving defect boxes and their positions. Confirm a real fault,
  reject a distraction and draw a missed fault. The inspector retains the decision.
- 1:10–1:45 — Show the buyer's 4-point score and evidence PDF. Every point has a size, location and reason.
  A supervisor can accept, claim or reject with an audit entry.
- 1:45–2:20 — Show lot decision, supplier scorecard and claim pack; then cutting skip zones, usable yards,
  supervisor approval and cutting-master acknowledgment.
- 2:20–2:45 — Show daily reference verification and a real-model benchmark beside the simulated baseline.
  Explicitly state misses. Four of seven faults found on the current demo roll is not factory-wide accuracy.
- 2:45–3:00 — Edge design keeps factory images local. The next validation is a paired manual/assisted pilot;
  do not claim measured savings that have not been collected.
