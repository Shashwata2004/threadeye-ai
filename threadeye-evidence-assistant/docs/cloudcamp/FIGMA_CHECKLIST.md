## Required frames and states

- [ ] Login plus role-aware navigation for inspector, supervisor and at least one downstream viewer.
- [ ] Dashboard with roll/status summaries, alerts and clear next actions.
- [ ] Receiving queue and new receiving record.
- [ ] Roll registration with supplier/lot, length, fabric construction and validation states.
- [ ] Generated QR identity and scan/lookup path.
- [ ] Inspection setup and live/recorded inspection progress.
- [ ] Proposed defect list/map with class, location and review status.
- [ ] Human defect accept/correct/reject flow with mandatory correction reason.
- [ ] Deterministic four-point score breakdown and disposition guidance, visually separate from model confidence.
- [ ] Supervisor evidence review, approve/reject/hold decision and reason.
- [ ] Reports/evidence pack with traceability, audit information and export state.
- [ ] Bangla and English representative screens.
- [ ] Empty, loading, validation, denied, offline/error and success states where meaningful.
- [ ] Desktop operational layout and at least one practical tablet/mobile review layout.

## Clickable prototype flow

- [ ] Start page opens at login; a demo credential/role choice is explained without a real password.
- [ ] Primary happy path links every frame: login → receive → register → QR → inspect → correct → score → supervisor
  → report.
- [ ] Back/cancel, validation errors, role denial and logout have meaningful destinations.
- [ ] Supervisor and inspector journeys diverge where authorization differs.
- [ ] Hotspots are attached to real controls; no invisible full-screen “next” overlays.
- [ ] A reviewer can return to dashboard/navigation without restarting the prototype.

## Design-quality and truth checks

- [ ] Components, typography, spacing, colors, tables, badges, forms and dialogs use a consistent design system.
- [ ] Color is not the only signal; focus, contrast, labels and target sizes support accessibility.
- [ ] The score is called deterministic; detection confidence and the four-point decision are not conflated.
- [ ] The hosted/simulated and local/trained-model states are labeled accurately when depicted.
- [ ] No real buyer data, private credentials, unsupported ROI, fake “live AI” animation or fabricated production
  result appears.
- [ ] Fictional sample identifiers are clearly recognizable as demonstration data.
